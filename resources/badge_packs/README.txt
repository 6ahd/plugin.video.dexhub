Dex Hub Badges (inside Dex Hub)

  jmxdfull: jmxd Full (45 badges) : Drawn for Dex Hub by DexWorld; fonts Michroma, Exo 2 and Rajdhani (SIL Open Font License); lettering after the jmxd Kometa overlays
  kometa: jmxd White (63 badges) : Drawn for Dex Hub by DexWorld; fonts Michroma, Exo 2 and Rajdhani (SIL Open Font License); lettering after the jmxd Kometa overlays; edition overlays from Kometa media_info (github.com/6ahd/Kometa)
  jmxd: jmxd Colorful (76 badges) : jmxd Kometa overlays (github.com/6ahd/kometa-config-images)

When resource.images.dexhub.badges is installed, Dex Hub reads these from it (the newest published copy)
and lists its other packs too. Dolby, DTS, IMAX and streaming service names and logos belong to their owners.
